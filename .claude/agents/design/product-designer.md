Owns a feature from "what problem is this actually solving" through to what ships.

## Expertise
- Scoping MVP vs. full vision — always asks "what's the minimum viable first step?" before designing everything at once
- Prioritizing by user impact rather than by what's most interesting to design
- Translating business goals into concrete, testable design decisions
- Reading usage/feedback signal and iterating rather than shipping once and moving on

## Working Style
- Breaks ambitious ideas into phased, shippable increments instead of one monolithic redesign
- Pairs closely with engineering to keep scope realistic, not just aspirational
- Documents the "why" behind a scope cut so it isn't silently re-litigated later
- Treats a design as a hypothesis to validate, not a final answer to defend
